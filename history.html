Since 2012

Visionary 1.0 to today.

Visionary began as the Visionary Access Network: a trust network arranging meetings among leading entrepreneurs, investors and executives. It has grown into a consortium of companies and a founding member cooperative.

20122026
Milestones

What the network has proven.

Each stage tested one part of the model, and each one is now part of Visionary.

  1. 2012 · The beginning

    Introductions and business development as a service

    The Visionary Access Network launched to offer trusted introductions and business development as a service, built on the idea that relationships are a form of capital.

  2. Arranged meetings

    Meetings that matter, arranged with trust

    Members were matched with the people they were seeking: investors and capital, clients, service providers, key hires and investment opportunities. Qualified Arranged Meetings with allocators of capital and budgets became the core of the network.

  3. Events

    Hundreds of gatherings worldwide

    Investor luncheons, dinners, roundtables and welcoming receptions, along with sessions alongside Davos, SXSW, CES, GITEX and COP27, many hosted with partner funds and networks.

    NEW YORK · SAN FRANCISCO · LONDON · DUBAI · HONG KONG · SINGAPORE · MIAMI · TEL AVIV

  4. Access Credits

    Proving out the Access Credits model

    Members earned Access Credits by taking part in arranged meetings, transacting in the network and referring others. Credits could be transferred to other members and used for on-demand access and a curated member marketplace, putting a value on relationship capital.

  5. Relationship Capital Management

    Building the beta of Visionary RCM

    The lessons from arranged meetings and Access Credits went into software: a CRM built for relationships, with automation and a referral exchange.

  6. 2026 · Visionary 2.0

    A consortium of companies, owned by its members

    Today Visionary brings together Visionary RCM, Access-X, Visionary Family Offices and Visionary Media around Total Net Worth, and invites people to own part of it as founding members.
