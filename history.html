Our story

Visionary 1.0 to today.

Visionary began as the Visionary Access Network: a trust network arranging meetings among leading entrepreneurs, investors and executives. It has grown into a consortium of companies and a founding member cooperative.

The beginningToday
01 · The beginning

It started with a paintbrush.

The Visionary name was first painted by hand on a New York rooftop, on a studio wall and on champagne bottles for the first gatherings. That brushstroke is still the logo.

The Visionary Access Network launched to offer trusted introductions and business development as a service, built on the idea that relationships are a form of capital.

Milestones

What the network has proven.

Each stage tested one part of the model, and each one is now part of Visionary. Click any picture to see it whole.

  1. 02 · First gatherings

    Receptions, forums and salons

    Summits, investor welcoming sessions and salon presentations brought entrepreneurs, investors and executives into the same room.

    • Wall Street Green Summit reception: co-hosted with Gentry Venture Partners
    • Visionary NYC Forum: keynote Jack Hidary, MC Glenn Toby
    • Visionary Spotlight: salon presentations
    • Princeton Social Enterprise Network: featured speakers and partners
  2. 03 · Arranged meetings

    Meetings that matter, arranged with trust

    Members were matched with the people they were seeking: investors and capital, clients, service providers, key hires and investment opportunities. Qualified Arranged Meetings with allocators of capital and budgets became the core of the network.

    • Investor roundtables, Los Angeles: Meet Your Match welcoming reception
    • Welcoming reception, Washington, DC: hosted with Dr. Glenn Toby
    • WAIS Bermuda, powered by Visionary: World Alternative Investment Summit
  3. 04 · Access Credits

    Proving out the Access Credits model

    Members earned Access Credits by taking part in arranged meetings, transacting in the network and referring others. Credits could be transferred to other members and used for on-demand access and a curated member marketplace, putting a value on relationship capital.

    • Access Credit Supernova: NYC Blockchain Week
    • By-invitation roundtable, Davos: during the World Economic Forum
    • #HODL #AccessCredits: Access Credits campaign
    • End of year gatherings: Montréal, Toronto, Los Angeles and NYC
  4. 05 · Access, Grow, Own

    Arranged meetings go virtual

    Welcoming receptions for alumni communities, health and business networks brought new invitees in, with Access Credits issued along the way.

    • Princeton Tigers welcoming reception: launching Access, Grow, Own
    • Health welcoming reception: featuring xCures
    • Summer "Meet Your Match": co-hosted with C-Suite Network
  5. 06 · Powered by Arranged Meetings

    Hundreds of gatherings worldwide

    Investor luncheons, dinners, roundtables and welcoming receptions, along with sessions alongside Davos, SXSW, CES, GITEX and COP27, many hosted with partner funds and networks.

    NEW YORK · SAN FRANCISCO · LONDON · DUBAI · HONG KONG · SINGAPORE · MIAMI · TEL AVIV

    • Davos Longevity Gathering: powered by Arranged Meetings
    • Diversity, Equity & Inclusion gathering: during Black History Month
    • CC Forum Dubai announcement: powered by Arranged Meetings
    • Hemp Financing Solutions: A Graham Advisors, powered by Visionary
  6. 07 · Relationship Capital Management

    Building the beta of Visionary RCM

    The lessons from arranged meetings and Access Credits went into software: a CRM built for relationships, with automation and a referral exchange.

    See how RCM works →
  7. 08 · Today · Visionary 2.0

    A consortium of companies, owned by its members

    Today Visionary brings together Visionary RCM, Access-X, Visionary Family Offices and Visionary Media around Total Net Worth, and invites people to own part of it as founding members.
