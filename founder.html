Total Net Worth

Wealth and health, measured together.

Through Total Net Worth, an individual's value can be measured and transferred across networks. Costs are absorbed rather than passed on, so the total possible value is maximized for an individual, an organization or a society.

Maximizing that value is the conviction behind all of the work: when value moves through trusted networks and its costs are accounted for, individuals, organizations and society can all come out ahead.

The approach is mathematical, analytical and objective. Longevity, for example, is a calculated objective: more healthy years extend every other part of Total Net Worth, from finances and relationships to the ability to make a lasting impact.

That is the logic that ties wealth and health together, and why Visionary is built around all three capitals: human, relationship and financial.
