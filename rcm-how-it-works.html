Overview

Master your network.

RCM moves your relationships through four stages, from scattered contact lists to an actively managed network.

Steps 01–02

Discover & Consolidate

Define goals, inventory contact sources and import the data. Reconcile duplicates into one customized CRM.

Step 03

Enrich & Score

Memory recall and AI research capture hidden context on each contact. Each contact is scored 1 to 10 for strategic fit.

Step 04

Activate & Manage

Run outreach campaigns, scheduling and interactions. A support team runs daily routines, checklists and regular reviews with you.

Part 1 · Discover, Consolidate, Enrich

Consolidating your current relationships and contacts.

Your relationships live in many places. The first job is to bring them together, then decide which ones matter most right now.

Export your contacts from any platform

Every source exports to a portable file (CSV, Excel or vCard), which becomes the raw material for your CRM.

Messaging

WhatsApp

Use the WASendly extension on web.whatsapp.com to extract chats or saved contacts as CSV or Excel.

Social

LinkedIn

Request the larger data archive in Settings & Privacy, wait for the email, then unzip connections.csv.

Cloud

Google Workspace

Select contacts at contacts.google.com, click More Actions > Export, and choose CSV or vCard.

Device

Apple iOS

Highlight all contacts in the Mac Contacts app or iCloud.com, then export as vCard.

Prioritize and score: the review session

A step-by-step process to identify, rank and score your highest-potential relationships. Do it yourself, or have us run it with you.

Step 1

Set up one-hour sessions

Scroll through exports name by name and record notes by video, audio, AI or a live assistant.

Step 2

Rate and tag priority contacts

Evaluate key relationships on three measures and assign them to specific campaign targets.

Step 3

Work in iterative passes

Consolidate top-tier standouts first, then work systematically through lower-priority batches.

MeasureQuestionNotation
TrustHow well do you know them?Deeply known · Generally known
CaliberHow significant is their potential?Very significant · Significant
UrgencyHow soon should you activate them?High · Medium · Later

Part 2 · Activate

Managing your ongoing relationships and contacts.

Traditional relationship management fails at follow-through. Context stays trapped in inbox notes, and traditional CRMs add manual entry nobody has time for.

Attended automation for busy executives

RCM runs on a Google Sheets database powered by AI speed and managed by human judgment. Agentic AI (Claude or ChatGPT) handles the high-volume processing: it parses notes, enriches context, prioritizes contacts and drafts outreach.

Your team and routines

A customizable human team works in tandem with you. Nothing goes out without your approval.

Team

Routine

Send a voice note or text; the team logs it and drafts the follow-up.

Routine

Daily

To-dos, drafts and meeting prep queued for your approval.

Routine

Regular review

A pipeline checklist: progress, stalled leads and re-engagement.

Part 3 · Expand

Expanding your network through the Access-X referral exchange.

Once your own network is organized, it becomes something you can exchange. Introductions you make earn credits; credits buy new, matched meetings.

Monetizing relationship capital: Access Credits

Earn credits for existing relationships

Beyond internal relationships: inter-network access

One opportunity, cross-network matching, and a pipeline of qualified meetings. An originating client shares an offering and target criteria (role, sector, location, need). Contacts stay in each Access Partner's own RCM; matches follow agreed criteria, and the model scales across networks.

  1. Prepare the matchClient criteria are matched against partner networks.
  2. Notify matched prospectsEach partner notifies its own matched contacts.
  3. Double opt-inClient and contact both accept. Declined or pending means no introduction.
  4. IntroduceShare context and arrange the meeting.
  5. Qualified meeting pipelineMultiple meetings for the originating client. Outcomes return to the client.
