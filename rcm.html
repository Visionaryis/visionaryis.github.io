How it works

Five stages. One continuous cycle.

Relationship Capital Management turns scattered contacts into a scored, prioritized network you can act on, and then grows it through trusted introductions.

  1. 01
    DiscoverDefine goals, inventory every contact source and import the data.
  2. 02
    ConsolidateReconcile duplicates into one customized CRM.
  3. 03
    EnrichMemory recall and AI research add context. Each contact is scored 1 to 10 for strategic fit.
  4. 04
    ActivateOutreach, scheduling and follow-up, with your approval before anything goes out.
  5. 05
    ExpandExchange trusted introductions across partner networks through Access-X.

Every interaction feeds the next discovery cycle

What it does

Organize. Automate. Exchange.

Three parts that turn a contact list into relationship capital.

01 · Organize

Prioritize and tag contacts

Bring your contacts together in one place, then rank and tag them so the right relationships stand out.

  • Export contacts from WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Google and Apple
  • One-hour review sessions, recorded by video, audio, AI or a live assistant
  • Rate each contact on Trust, Caliber and Urgency
Consolidating your relationships →
02 · Automate

Keep relationships moving

Automation handles the routine follow-up so you can focus on the people. We call it attended automation: AI speed, managed by human judgment.

  • A Google Sheets CRM, customized to you
  • AI parses notes, enriches context and drafts outreach
  • An associate and senior team member run the routine; you approve
Managing ongoing relationships →
03 · Exchange

Referral exchange

Trusted referrals move between members of the network, with Access-X matching people and opportunities.

  • Earn Access Credits when contacts you refer opt in
  • Spend credits on matched, double opt-in introductions
  • Matching runs across a network of partner networks
